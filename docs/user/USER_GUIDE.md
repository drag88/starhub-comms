## Introduction

### What is the StarHub Communications Generator?

The StarHub Communications Generator is an AI-powered tool that helps marketing teams create effective, compliant customer communications across multiple channels. Instead of writing communications from scratch, you provide campaign details and the system generates 5 professionally-crafted variations, automatically recommending the most effective option based on a 4-pillar scoring system.

### Who is this for?

- **Marketing Managers**: Creating campaign communications quickly
- **Content Creators**: Getting multiple creative variations
- **Campaign Coordinators**: Ensuring brand and compliance consistency
- **Product Marketers**: Launching promotions across channels

### Key Benefits

- **Save Time**: Generate 5 variations in seconds instead of hours of writing
- **Improve Quality**: AI-powered recommendations based on proven engagement patterns
- **Ensure Compliance**: Automatic validation against regulatory requirements
- **Multi-Channel**: Optimized formatting for Email, SMS, and Push Notifications
- **Consistency**: Maintain StarHub brand voice across all communications

## Creating a Campaign

Follow these steps to generate your first set of communications:

### Step 1: Choose Your Channel

**What is a Channel?**
The communication medium through which your message will be delivered.

**Available Channels:**

**📧 Email**
- Best for: Detailed promotions, newsletters, rich content
- Format: Subject line (40-60 characters) + body text
- Features: Can include links, formatted text, images
- Example use: Monthly promotion announcements, product launches

**📱 SMS**
- Best for: Urgent updates, time-sensitive offers, service notifications
- Format: Single message up to 160 characters
- Features: Direct, immediate delivery
- Example use: Flash sales, service alerts, appointment reminders

**🔔 Push Notification**
- Best for: App engagement, real-time updates, quick actions
- Format: Title (40-50 chars) + Body (100-120 chars)
- Features: Instant visibility, high urgency
- Example use: Limited-time offers, breaking news, event reminders

**How to Select:**
1. Click on the channel dropdown
2. Choose Email, SMS, or Push Notification
3. The form will adjust to show channel-specific requirements

### Step 4: Define Campaign Objective

**What is an Objective?**
The primary goal of your communication - what action you want customers to take.

**Available Objectives:**

1. **Promotion**
   - Goal: Drive new sales
   - Tone: Exciting, value-focused
   - Example: "Limited time offer: 50% off first 3 months"

2. **Retention**
   - Goal: Keep existing customers
   - Tone: Appreciative, exclusive

3. **Upsell**
   - Goal: Upgrade to higher-tier service
   - Tone: Aspirational, benefit-focused
   - Example: "Upgrade to Premium and get 4K streaming"

4. **Cross-sell**
   - Goal: Add complementary services
   - Tone: Convenient, complementary
   - Example: "Add Sports+ to your plan for just $15/mth"

5. **Service Update**
   - Goal: Inform about changes
   - Tone: Professional, informative
   - Example: "Network maintenance tonight 1-3AM"

6. **Onboarding**
   - Goal: Welcome new customers
   - Tone: Friendly, helpful
   - Example: "Welcome to StarHub! Here's how to get started"

### Step 5: Enter Promotion Details

**What to Include:**

This is the core message content - what you're offering or announcing.

**Best Practices:**

✅ **Do Include:**
- Specific offer details (e.g., "$200 discount")
- Duration (e.g., "3 months free")
- Key benefits (e.g., "Includes Netflix Premium")
- Terms (e.g., "24-month contract")
- Unique value propositions

❌ **Don't Include:**
- Generic statements without details
- Unsubstantiated claims
- Overly complex terms
- Multiple unrelated offers

**Examples:**

**Good Promotion Details:**
```
$200 discount on HomeHub+ with 3 months free Netflix Premium.
24-month contract required. Normal price $135.66/mth, now $115.66/mth.
```

**Service Update Details:**
```
Network maintenance scheduled for tonight 1AM-3AM in Central region.
Services may be temporarily unavailable. No action required.
```

**Retention Offer Details:**
```
Thank you for being a loyal customer! Enjoy exclusive 20GB bonus data
for the next 3 months at no extra cost. No action needed.
```

**How to Enter:**
1. Click in the "Promotion Details" text box
2. Type or paste your offer details
3. Include all relevant information the AI should use
4. Be specific but concise

### Step 8: Specify Required Phrases (Optional)

**What are Required Phrases?**
Specific text that MUST appear in every generated variation.

**When to Use:**
- Legal requirements ("T&Cs apply")
- Brand campaigns (" exclusive offer")
- Specific pricing ("$115.66/mth")
- Product names ("Premier League")
- Campaign taglines

**Format:**
Enter phrases separated by commas: `phrase1, phrase2, phrase3`

**Examples:**

```
exclusive offer, $115.66/mth
```

```
Premier League, Sports+
```

```
family plan, unlimited data
```

```
T&Cs apply, while stocks last
```

**Important Notes:**
- Phrases are case-sensitive
- Exact match required in all variations
- Keep phrases concise and specific
- Maximum recommended: 3-4 required phrases

**How to Enter:**
1. Click in "Required Phrases" field (optional)
2. Type first phrase
3. Add comma, then next phrase
4. Repeat for all required phrases

---

### Step 9: List Prohibited Words (Optional)

**What are Prohibited Words?**
Words or phrases that should NEVER appear in generated content.

**When to Use:**
- Competitor names
- Controversial terms
- Words that violate brand guidelines
- Legally restricted claims
- Inappropriate language

**Format:**
Enter words separated by commas: `word1, word2, word3`

**Examples:**

```
competitor, guarantee, unlimited
```

```
free (when not truly free), best, guaranteed
```

**Common Prohibited Terms:**
- Competitor brand names
- "Guarantee" (without substantiation)
- "Unlimited" (if there are fair usage limits)
- "Best" (unless proven by third-party)
- "Always", "Never" (absolutes)

**How to Enter:**
1. Click in "Prohibited Words" field (optional)
2. Type words to avoid, separated by commas
3. System will validate and regenerate if found

## Understanding Results

After generation completes, you'll see 5 communication variations with scores and recommendations.

### The 5 Variations

**Why 5 Variations?**
- Provides creative options
- Different messaging angles
- A/B testing opportunities
- Backup options if edits needed

**What Makes Them Different?**
- Subject lines / titles
- Opening hooks
- Benefit emphasis
- Call-to-action phrasing
- Sentence structure

All variations include:
- Required phrases (if specified)
- Promotion details
- Appropriate tone
- Channel-specific formatting
- Compliance elements

### Understanding the Recommendation Score

Each variation receives an **Overall Score (0-100)** combining 4 pillars:

**🎯 Cohort Alignment (30% weight)**
- How well the message resonates with selected cohorts
- Language and benefits tailored to audience preferences
- Personalization effectiveness

*Example: For "Deal Seekers", emphasizes savings and value*

**📱 Channel Optimization (25% weight)**
- Appropriate format for the chosen channel
- Optimal length and structure
- Technical requirements met

*Example: SMS stays under 160 characters, push has compelling title*

**⭐ Brand Consistency (25% weight)**
- Adherence to StarHub brand voice
- Professional quality
- Appropriate tone execution
- Trustworthy messaging

*Example: Maintains friendly-professional StarHub voice*

**🚀 Engagement Potential (20% weight)**
- Likelihood to drive customer action
- Strong call-to-action
- Compelling value proposition
- Urgency and relevance

*Example: Clear next step, compelling reason to act now*

**Overall Score Calculation:**
```
Overall = (Cohort × 0.30) + (Channel × 0.25) + (Brand × 0.25) + (Engagement × 0.20)
```

### Score Interpretation

**90-100**: Exceptional
- Highly optimized for all factors
- Recommended for immediate use
- Minimal editing needed

**80-89**: Excellent
- Strong across all pillars
- Minor refinements optional
- Ready for use

**70-79**: Good
- Solid performance
- May benefit from light editing
- Acceptable for most campaigns

**60-69**: Fair
- Acceptable baseline
- Consider regenerating or editing
- May need customization

**Below 60**: Needs Improvement
- Regenerate with adjusted inputs
- Significant editing required
- Review campaign parameters

### The Top Recommendation

**How It's Chosen:**
- Highest overall score among the 5 variations
- Highlighted with distinct visual indicator
- Marked as "Recommended"

**Should You Always Use the Top Recommendation?**

Usually, yes - but consider:

✅ **Use Top Recommendation When:**
- Score is 80+
- Message aligns with your vision
- Compliance notes are clean
- Tone feels right

🤔 **Consider Alternatives When:**
- You prefer a different messaging angle
- Specific phrase resonates better
- A/B testing multiple options
- Alternative better fits campaign theme

**Pro Tip:** The recommendation is AI-driven, but human judgment matters. Trust the score, but use your marketing expertise.

### Compliance Notes

**What Are Compliance Notes?**
Automated validation results checking for:
- Regulatory requirements
- Brand guidelines
- Channel-specific rules
- Legal disclaimers

**Possible Compliance Statuses:**

✅ **No Issues Detected**
- All compliance checks passed
- Ready for use
- No action required

⚠️ **Minor Suggestions**
- Non-critical recommendations
- Optional improvements
- Best practice reminders

❌ **Issues Found**
- Compliance violations detected
- Must be addressed before use
- Review and fix required

**Common Compliance Checks:**

**For SMS Promotional:**
- ✅ Contains opt-out language ("Reply STOP")
- ✅ Under 160 characters
- ✅ Clear sender identification

**For Email Promotional:**
- ✅ Includes unsubscribe link
- ✅ Subject line appropriate length
- ✅ Sender address compliant

**For All Channels:**
- ✅ Pricing includes terms ("/mth", "contract")
- ✅ "Free" offers mention conditions
- ✅ No unsubstantiated claims ("best", "guaranteed")
- ✅ Required disclaimers present ("T&Cs apply")

**How to Address Issues:**

1. Read compliance note details
2. Click "Edit" on the variation
3. Make required changes
4. Save and re-check
5. Or click "Regenerate" with adjusted inputs

## Editing Communications

Sometimes you'll want to refine a generated variation manually.

### When to Edit

✅ **Good Reasons to Edit:**
- Adjust specific wording preference
- Add campaign-specific detail
- Fine-tune character count
- Personalize for specific audience
- Fix minor compliance issue

❌ **Avoid Editing For:**
- Major content changes (regenerate instead)
- Different tone entirely (use tone selector)
- Complete rewrite (regenerate with better inputs)

### How to Edit

**Step-by-Step:**

1. **Select Variation**
   - Click on the variation you want to edit
   - Can be any of the 5, not just the recommendation

2. **Click "Edit" Button**
   - Opens edit modal overlay
   - Shows current content in editable fields

3. **Make Changes**

   **For Email:**
   - Edit Subject line (40-60 chars recommended)
   - Edit Body text
   - Character counts update in real-time

   **For SMS:**
   - Edit message (160 chars max)
   - Counter shows characters remaining
   - Warning if exceeding limit

   **For Push Notification:**
   - Edit Title (40-50 chars)
   - Edit Body (100-120 chars)
   - Both counters visible

4. **Review Changes**
   - Check character counts
   - Verify required phrases still present
   - Ensure compliance elements included

5. **Save**
   - Click "Save" button
   - Edit modal closes
   - Variation now shows "Edited" badge

6. **Cancel**
   - Click "Cancel" to discard changes
   - Original content preserved

### After Editing

**What Changes:**
- ✅ "Edited" badge appears on variation
- ✅ Modified text displayed
- ✅ Character count reflects new length
- ❌ Score NOT recalculated (shows original)

**Important Notes:**
- Original AI-generated text not stored in MVP
- Editing overwrites the variation
- To restore, regenerate the campaign
- Scores reflect pre-edit state

### Editing Best Practices

**Do:**
- ✅ Make minor refinements
- ✅ Respect channel character limits
- ✅ Maintain required phrases
- ✅ Keep compliance elements
- ✅ Preserve tone and style

**Don't:**
- ❌ Remove unsubscribe/opt-out links
- ❌ Delete pricing terms
- ❌ Exceed character limits
- ❌ Change core message completely
- ❌ Remove required phrases

## Exporting Communications

Once you've selected or edited your preferred communication, export it for use.

### Export Formats

**1. TXT (Plain Text)**
- Simple text file
- Human-readable
- Easy to copy-paste
- No formatting

**Use for:**
- Quick copying to email tools
- SMS platforms
- Simple documentation

**2. CSV (Comma-Separated Values)**
- Spreadsheet compatible
- Includes all metadata
- Good for bulk campaigns
- Multiple variations in one file

**Use for:**
- Importing to email marketing platforms
- Campaign tracking spreadsheets
- Archiving multiple variations
- Bulk operations

**3. JSON (Structured Data)**
- Machine-readable format
- Complete metadata
- API integration ready
- Preserves structure

**Use for:**
- System integrations
- API uploads
- Complete data preservation
- Technical workflows

### How to Export

**Exporting a Single Communication:**

1. **Select Variation**
   - Click on the variation to export
   - Can be any variation, not just recommended

2. **Click "Export"**
   - Export menu appears

3. **Choose Format**
   - Click "Export as TXT"
   - Or "Export as JSON"
   - Or "Export as CSV" (single row)

4. **Download**
   - File downloads to your browser's download folder
   - Filename includes communication ID

**Exporting All Variations:**

1. **Click "Export All"**
   - Located near top of results section

2. **Choose Format**
   - Best format: CSV (all variations in rows)
   - JSON also available (array of communications)

3. **Download**
   - Single file with all 5 variations
   - Includes scores and metadata

### Export File Contents

**TXT Format Example:**
```

[Body content here]

```

123,email,"Subject here","Body here",85,90,88,82,87,false

  "id": 123,

    "cohort_alignment": 85,

## Best Practices

### Campaign Creation Best Practices

**1. Be Specific in Promotion Details**
✅ Good: "$200 discount on 24-month HomeHub+ contract, includes Netflix Premium"
❌ Bad: "Great deal on broadband"

**2. Choose Cohorts Strategically**
- Select cohorts that genuinely match your target
- Don't select all cohorts (weakens targeting)
- 1-3 cohorts is optimal

**3. Match Tone to Objective and Audience**
- Retention → Appreciative
- Promotion → Excited or Urgent
- Service Update → Professional
- High-value customers → Premium

**4. Use Custom Instructions Wisely**
- Add context AI wouldn't know
- Specify time-sensitive details
- Mention regional targeting
- Keep instructions clear and concise

**5. Leverage Required Phrases**
- Legal requirements (T&Cs)
- Campaign taglines
- Specific pricing
- Brand campaign tie-ins

### Generation Best Practices

**1. Generate First, Edit Later**
- Let AI create variations
- Review all 5 before editing
- Minor edits only (major changes = regenerate)

**2. Trust the Recommendation (Usually)**
- Top score chosen for good reasons
- But use judgment if another variation fits better
- Consider A/B testing top 2 scores

**3. Regenerate When Needed**
- If none of the 5 are satisfactory
- After changing campaign parameters
- To get fresh creative angles

### Compliance Best Practices

**1. Always Review Compliance Notes**
- Even with green status
- Especially for promotional campaigns
- Before exporting for use

**2. Verify Required Elements**

**SMS Promotional:**
- Opt-out instructions
- Character limit adherence

**Email Promotional:**
- Unsubscribe link
- Sender identification

**All Channels:**
- Pricing terms
- Disclaimers
- Truthful claims

**3. Edit Carefully**
- Don't remove compliance elements
- Maintain required phrases
- Keep within character limits

### Channel-Specific Best Practices

**Email Best Practices:**
- Subject line clarity > cleverness
- Front-load key benefit
- Clear call-to-action
- Mobile-friendly content
- Unsubscribe prominently placed

**SMS Best Practices:**
- Brevity is critical
- Lead with value
- Clear next action
- URL shorteners if needed
- Respect opt-out requests

**Push Notification Best Practices:**
- Title must grab attention
- Body should create urgency
- Time-sensitive offers work best
- Clear value proposition
- Single, clear action

## Troubleshooting

### Generation Issues

**Problem: "Generation failed" error**

**Possible Causes:**
- Backend server not running
- API key invalid or expired
- Network connectivity issue

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Check internet connection
3. Try again in a few moments
4. Contact IT support if persists

---

**Problem: Generated content doesn't match expectations**

**Solutions:**
1. Review your promotion details - be more specific
2. Add custom instructions with context
3. Try a different tone
4. Regenerate with adjusted inputs
5. Use required phrases to guide AI

---

**Problem: All variations seem too similar**

**Explanation:** AI creates variations with different angles, but they'll share core message.

**Solutions:**
1. Regenerate - sometimes get different creative approaches
2. Provide more diverse promotion details
3. Add custom instructions requesting specific angles
4. Accept that variations will be similar if inputs are narrow

---

### Compliance Issues

**Problem: Compliance warning appears**

**Solutions:**
1. Read the specific compliance note
2. Click "Edit" on the flagged variation
3. Add missing elements (opt-out, terms, etc.)
4. Or regenerate with compliance requirements in custom instructions

---

**Problem: Required phrase missing from variation**

**This shouldn't happen** - if it does:
1. Click "Regenerate"
2. Verify required phrases field is filled correctly
3. If persists, contact support

---

### Export Issues

**Problem: Export download doesn't start**

**Solutions:**
1. Check browser's download settings
2. Allow pop-ups from the application
3. Try a different browser
4. Try a different export format

---

**Problem: Can't open exported file**

**Solutions:**
- TXT: Open with any text editor (Notepad, TextEdit)
- CSV: Open with Excel, Google Sheets, or text editor
- JSON: Open with text editor or JSON viewer

**Q: Can I customize the number of variations?**
A: Not in MVP - fixed at 5 variations.

### Scoring Questions

**Q: Why isn't my preferred variation scored highest?**

**Q: Do scores change if I edit?**
A: No, scores reflect the original AI-generated content, not edits.

**Q: What's a "good" score?**
A: 80+ is excellent, 70-79 is good, 60-69 is fair. Below 60, consider regenerating.

**Q: Can I see how scores are calculated?**
A: Click "View Score Breakdown" on any variation to see the 4-pillar breakdown.
